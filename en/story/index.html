CHAPTER 01–03 · TINY DEFENSE

A Castle to Come Home To

CHAPTER 01

A Castle Above the Seal

Long ago, people built a castle over the seal that held the sleeping monsters. No one looked beneath it.

One night, the seal cracked. Monsters slipped out and battered the walls. Each tremor widened the breach.

The Weight of an Empty Sack

The boy’s timber braced the gate. After a warm meal, the soldiers returned to the walls.

Pawn
But all I did was bring back wood…

Old man
That wood kept the gate standing. You helped defend us too.

Old man
Will you gather supplies for us, like you learned yesterday?

Pawn
Yes, I’ll try!

You Need Not Stand Alone

Pawn
If I make the ring, could I fight like you?

Old man
You borrow a hero’s power, child. You do not give them control of your body.

Pawn
Can the hero speak to me, too?

Old man
Yes. I heard a voice from my sword, too. But the choices are yours to make.

Old man
The castle held even when my sword broke. There were people on the walls, and people below them.

After the Sword Broke

The morning after the broken-toothed troll fell, the old man quietly ran his hand over the gate.

Pawn
We held them off, even without your sword.

Old man
Yes. The sword broke, but the will that protected us lives on.

Against the Tide

Pawn
If they came this way, what lies at the other end?

Old man
I don’t know, child. I spent my whole life guarding the gate.

Pawn
Then let’s find out together. There might be a way to stop them.

Paths in the Stone

Beyond spring stood an old waystone. Its carved paths branched outward from a single castle.

Old man
I thought there was only one seal to guard… I never knew so many paths led to it.

Pawn
Let’s find the path to our castle first.

What Must Not Be Passed On

The same mark lay beneath fallen leaves. The old man thought of his years on the walls.

Old man
I’m glad you’ve grown stronger. But I don’t want you to spend your whole life on those walls too.

Pawn
Neither do I. When we get home, I want us both to rest.

From the Other Side

At winter’s waystone, the lantern lit the last path. The seal at home could be closed from this side too.

Old man
Once we weather this winter, we can go home. We’ll close the path behind us.

Pawn
Yes. This time, let’s give our castle a chance to rest.

Today, You Can Rest

After they returned, the wisps’ light sealed one path. For once, a quiet morning came to the castle gates.

Pawn
What should I bring back today?

Old man
Today, you can rest.

Other paths still remained. Before night came again, they sat together and shared a loaf of bread.

CHAPTER 02

Beneath the Seal

Pawn
We closed a path, but I still hear something underground. Did the seal break again?

Old man
We closed one path to the castle. The great seal beneath it is still damaged.

Pawn
Then let’s find out how they sealed it the first time. We can start with the castle records!

When the Earth Split

Old man
The old records tell of people living alongside spirits. Then one day, the earth shook.

Pawn
Why? Did someone split the earth open?

Old man
The records do not say why. Monsters poured out of the cracks, and the soldiers grew weary of endless fighting.

A Power Shared

Old man
To help the people, the Spirit King gathered all his strength and shared it with the kingdom’s defenders.

Pawn
So it wasn’t just people who fought with swords?

Old man
That’s right. Knights with shields, and those who wielded magic. They became the heroes we know.

What Lies Beneath

Old man
The heroes drove the monsters back and sealed the rift together. The kingdom built this castle to guard the seal.

Pawn
So when we repair the gate, we’re protecting the seal too.

Old man
Yes. The soldiers on the walls and you with your sack are protecting the same thing.

The Wound Remains

Pawn
It took several heroes to seal it. So one sword could never repair it all.

Old man
Yes. But the path you closed gave us time to breathe. Let’s look through the remaining records.

Pawn
I’ll help defend it tonight, too. Now I know why this castle stands here.

CHAPTER 03

A Name Remembered

Old man
Last year’s records mentioned Mars. It is time I told you my part. I was his apprentice.

Pawn
The strongest knight in the kingdom? You learned from him?

Old man
Yes. He taught me to hold a sword, and to get back up after a fall. I looked up to him.

The Knight Who Sought Glory

Old man
My master often spoke of the old heroes. He said he was waiting for his chance to save the kingdom.

Pawn
But isn’t it good when there’s no need to fight?

Old man
I think so too. But he told me he feared that, in peace, all his efforts would be forgotten.

The Second Fracture

Old man
That night I followed him below the castle. I saw him drive his sword into the seal. He said he would stop the monsters himself and become a hero.

Pawn
The troll came through the crack he made…?

Old man
Yes. The ancient troll was stronger than him. I led people out, then lost sight of my master beyond the falling stones.

When the Sword Answered

Old man
The monsters returned every night. When I picked up an old sword in the armoury, I heard a voice.

Pawn
It was the Guardian. He must still remember you as you were back then.

Old man
With his power, I drove the troll back. His consciousness answered from the sword, but I chose to stand at the gate.

Tomorrow, Together

Old man
I did not stay just to make amends for my master. People lived behind this gate. And so the decades passed.

Pawn
The sword broke, but you’re still here. So am I, and the soldiers. This time we’ll guard it together.

Old man
Yes. I won’t leave you with just a sword and endless nights. Tomorrow, we’ll open the gate together.
