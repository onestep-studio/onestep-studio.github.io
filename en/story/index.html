CHAPTER 01–03 · TINY DEFENSE

A Castle to Come Home To

CHAPTER 01

Prologue

Long ago, people built a castle over the seal that held the sleeping monsters. No one looked beneath it.

Prologue

One night, the seal cracked. Monsters slipped out and battered the walls. Each tremor widened the breach.

Prologue

The troll fled into the dark, a tooth broken. The old man’s sword had snapped too. At sunrise, the monsters retreated into the forest.

Prologue

The defenders slept, exhausted. Only a boy too young to wield a spear was left to gather supplies.

Prologue

The seal kept widening. The boy left the gate with an empty sack. Before sunset, he had to bring back enough for the castle to last another day.

The Weight of an Empty Sack

The boy’s timber braced the gate. After a warm meal, the soldiers returned to the walls.

Pawn
But all I did was bring back wood…

Old man
Your wood kept the gate standing. You helped keep us safe.

Old man
Will you bring us more wood and food today?

Pawn
Yes, I’ll try!

You Need Not Stand Alone

Pawn
If I make the ring, could I fight like you?

Old man
Yes. You can draw on a hero’s power to fight, just as I did.

Pawn
Can I talk to the hero in the ring, too?

Old man
Yes. The voice in my sword helped me, too. But how you fight is for you to decide.

Old man
And don’t try to hold them all off alone. When my sword broke, the people in the castle stood together.

After the Sword Broke

The morning after the broken-toothed troll fell, the old man quietly ran his hand over the gate.

Pawn
We held them off, even without your sword.

Old man
Yes. Now we can defend the castle together, even without my sword.

Against the Tide

Pawn
Monster tracks. Where do you think this path leads?

Old man
I’ve never come this far either. I always fought them at the castle gate.

Pawn
Then let’s go together. Maybe we can block the way they use to reach us.

Paths in the Stone

Beyond spring stood an old waystone. Its carved paths branched outward from a single castle.

Old man
All these paths lead to our castle. I was so focused on the seal beneath it that I never knew.

Pawn
Then let’s check them one by one. There must be a path we can close.

What Must Not Be Passed On

The same mark lay beneath fallen leaves. The old man thought of his years on the walls.

Old man
I can really count on you now. Still, I hope you won’t spend your whole life guarding the walls as I did.

Pawn
Then you have to rest too. Let’s make sure we both can when we get home.

From the Other Side

The spirit lit up the snow-covered waystone. Carved into it was a way to close the path leading home.

Old man
If we close this path, the monsters will have to turn back too. Let’s seal it once we’re safely across.

Pawn
Yes. Then the people at the castle can rest a little too, right?

Today, You Can Rest

After they returned, the wisps’ light sealed one path. For once, a quiet morning came to the castle gates.

Today, You Can Rest

Pawn
What should I bring back today?

Old man
Today, you can rest.

Other paths still remained. Before night came again, they sat together and shared a loaf of bread.

CHAPTER 02

Beneath the Seal

Pawn
We closed a path, but I still hear something underground. Did the seal break again?

Old man
We can’t tell yet. We closed one path, but we haven’t fully repaired the seal beneath the castle.

Pawn
How did they make the seal in the first place? Let’s look for records in the castle.

When the Earth Split

Old man
The old records tell of people living alongside spirits. Then one day, the earth shook.

Pawn
Why? Did someone split the earth open?

Old man
The records do not say why. Monsters poured out of the cracks, and the soldiers grew weary of endless fighting.

A Power Shared

Old man
The Spirit King shared his power with the kingdom’s defenders. As it took hold, their bodies grew smaller and their faces younger.

A Power Shared

Pawn
Were all the people who received that power knights?

Old man
No. There were knights with shields, and mages too. They became the heroes of the old stories.

What Lies Beneath

Old man
The heroes drove the monsters back and sealed the rift together. The kingdom built this castle to guard the seal.

Pawn
So when we repair the gate, we’re protecting the seal too.

Old man
That’s right. With the soldiers holding the walls and you bringing supplies, we can keep the seal safe too.

The Wound Remains

Pawn
So it took several heroes working together. Closing one path isn’t the end for us either.

Old man
But closing that path gave everyone a chance to breathe. We’ll find a way to deal with the rest together.

Pawn
I’ll help defend it tonight, too. Now I know why this castle stands here.

CHAPTER 03

A Name Remembered

Old man
Now I’ll tell you about the day the seal was broken again. It concerns my old master, the knight Mars.

Pawn
The strongest knight in the kingdom? He was your teacher?

Old man
Yes. He taught me to hold a sword, and to get back up after a fall. I looked up to him.

The Knight Who Sought Glory

Old man
My master often spoke of the old heroes. He said he was waiting for his chance to save the kingdom.

Pawn
But isn’t it good when there’s no need to fight?

Old man
I think so too. But my master said that without a battle, no one would see the skill he’d spent his life building.

The Second Fracture

Old man
One night, I followed him below the castle. As he drove his sword into the seal, he said he would slay the monsters that came out and become a hero.

Pawn
He let the monsters out on purpose… just to become a hero?

The Second Fracture

Old man
Yes. But even he couldn’t stop the ancient troll. While I was leading people to safety, I lost sight of him behind the falling stones.

When the Sword Answered

Old man
The monsters returned every night. When I picked up an old sword in the armoury, I heard a voice.

Pawn
Was that the Guardian’s voice? Did he help you?

Old man
Yes. With his power still in the sword, I drove the troll back. I couldn’t fully mend the seal, but I could protect the people.

Tomorrow, Together

Old man
Thinking of my master weighed on me. But I stayed because I wanted to protect the people here. Before I knew it, decades had passed.

Pawn
You don’t have to do it alone anymore. I’m here, and so are the soldiers. Let’s defend it together.

Old man
Yes, together. Knowing you’re all here puts my mind at ease.
