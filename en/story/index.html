CHAPTER 01 · TINY DEFENSE

A Castle to Come Home To

A Castle Above the Seal

Long ago, people built a castle over the seal that held the sleeping monsters. No one looked beneath it.

One night, the seal cracked. Monsters slipped out and battered the walls. Each tremor widened the breach.

The Weight of an Empty Sack

The boy’s timber braced the gate. After a warm meal, the soldiers returned to the walls.

Boy
But all I did was bring back wood…

Old man
That wood kept the gate standing. You helped defend us too.

Old man
Will you gather supplies for us, like you learned yesterday?

Boy
Yes, I’ll try!

You Need Not Stand Alone

Boy
If I make the ring, could I fight like you?

Old man
You could borrow that strength. But don’t try to hold them off alone.

Old man
The castle held even when my sword broke. There were people on the walls, and people below them.

After the Sword Broke

The morning after the broken-toothed troll fell, the old man quietly ran his hand over the gate.

Boy
We held them off, even without your sword.

Old man
Yes. Perhaps I can finally lay that sword to rest.

Against the Tide

Boy
If they came this way, what lies at the other end?

Old man
I don’t know, child. I spent my whole life guarding the gate.

Boy
Then let’s find out together. There might be a way to stop them.

Paths in the Stone

Beyond spring stood an old waystone. Its carved paths branched outward from a single castle.

Old man
I thought there was only one seal to guard… I never knew so many paths led to it.

Boy
Let’s find the path to our castle first.

What Must Not Be Passed On

The same mark lay beneath fallen leaves. The old man thought of his years on the walls.

Old man
I’m glad you’ve grown stronger. But I don’t want you to spend your whole life on those walls too.

Boy
Neither do I. When we get home, I want us both to rest.

From the Other Side

At winter’s waystone, the lantern lit the last path. The seal at home could be closed from this side too.

Old man
Once we weather this winter, we can go home. We’ll close the path behind us.

Boy
Yes. This time, let’s give our castle a chance to rest.

Today, You Can Rest

After they returned, the wisps’ light sealed one path. For once, a quiet morning came to the castle gates.

Boy
What should I bring back today?

Old man
Today, you can rest.

Other paths still remained. Before night came again, they sat together and shared a loaf of bread.
