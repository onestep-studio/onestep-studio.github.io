THE GAME

Prepare by day.
Survive the night.

Gather resources in the forest, grow your village and army, and defend your castle from the monsters that arrive each night.

Tiny Defense brings gathering, unit placement, and auto-battles to a one-handed portrait screen. Every small choice you make during the day shapes your chances of surviving the night.

DAY & NIGHT

A day's choices
shape the next night.

Everything you gather and build by day becomes the line that holds at night. Follow one full cycle across ten scenes.

01 / 05

Day — Prepare

  1. 01

    Gathering Rhythm

    Swing the moment the timing gauge reaches the green band. Land it perfectly and the streak builds, raising every harvest that follows.

  2. 02

    Ore Memory

    Remember where the vein flashed, then strike the same spots again. Keep the order and deeper ore opens up.

  3. 03

    Tool Growth

    Pour resources into your axe and pickaxe so a single swing yields more. The shorter the day, the more your tools earn for you.

  4. 04

    A Day in the Village

    Walk a spring afternoon while villagers go about their work. Every new building is one more reason the night holds.

  5. 05

    Relic Draft

    Pick one of three relics. That single choice reshapes every night that comes after.

  6. To night →

Night — Defend

  1. ← To day

  2. 01

    Forming the Line

    Drag spearmen, archers, and monks into the positions you want, then close the gaps from the tower shop.

  3. 02

    Holding the Line

    Guardians and soldiers meet the skeleton horde head-on. While the front rank holds, arrows fall from behind.

  4. 03

    Hero Skill

    The hero's spin attack clears everything within reach. When you spend it decides where the wave breaks.

  5. 04

    Boss Duel

    Every fifth night, chain the on-screen commands and face the boss yourself. Drop the input and the counterattack lands.

  6. 05

    Beyond the Season

    Past spring waits summer, and past that, fall and winter. Every season turn pushes the night one step deeper.
